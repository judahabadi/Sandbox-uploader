Sandbox Uploader

A personal publishing tool for the YouTube channel Sandbox (@AtSandbox): AI tools, automation workflows and AI income systems, explained visually.

What it does

  • Uploads videos made by the channel owner from their own Google Drive to their own YouTube channel, with title, description, tags and thumbnail. Videos upload as unlisted so the owner can review them before making them public in YouTube Studio.
  • Updates the channel's description, keywords and banner.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. It is not available to other people or channels.
