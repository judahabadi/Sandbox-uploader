Sandbox Uploader – Privacy Policy

Effective: November 18, 2026 · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Sandbox Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Sandbox (@AtSandbox). It is not offered to the public and has no other users. This policy explains what the app accesses, how it is used, and how to remove it.

1. YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and manage channel branding. By using the app, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

2. Google Privacy Policy

Data handled by Google and YouTube is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

3. Information the app accesses, collects and uses

  • YouTube channel data: the channel ID and branding settings (description, keywords, banner), read and updated only for the owner's own channel.
  • Videos and metadata: video files, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and tags the owner places in their own Google Drive, uploaded to the owner's own channel (as unlisted).
  • Google Drive: files in the owner's folders "Shorts Queue", "Shorts Posted" and "Channel Settings"; the app writes small status files (results and errors) back to those folders.

The app does not access any other user's data, comments, subscribers, analytics or monetization data.

4. Storage and sharing

All data stays inside the owner's own Google account (Drive and YouTube). The app has no server, database or external storage. No data is sold, rented, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

5. Cookies

The app does not place, access or recognize cookies on any device, and these web pages set no cookies or trackers.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (remove "Sandbox Uploader").

7. Data deletion

The app keeps no copies of data outside the owner's account. To delete data the app created, delete the folders "Shorts Queue", "Shorts Posted" and "Channel Settings" in Google Drive; uploaded videos can be deleted in YouTube Studio. On request, any data related to the app is deleted within 7 days, and access is revoked as described in section 6. Data obtained through YouTube API Services is never retained beyond what is needed to complete an upload.

8. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: via the Sandbox YouTube channel (@AtSandbox) About page.
