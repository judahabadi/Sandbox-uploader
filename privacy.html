Sandbox Uploader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Sandbox Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Sandbox (@AtSandbox). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

  • Google Drive: reads video files, thumbnails and text (title, description, tags) that the channel owner places in their own Drive folders, and writes small status files (results and errors) back to those folders.
  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): uploads videos to the owner's own channel, sets their thumbnails, and updates the channel's description, keywords and banner.

What it stores

Nothing outside the owner's own Google account. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The tool keeps no database and no copies of data on any other server.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy applies to data handled by Google.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (remove "Sandbox Uploader"). Data the tool created stays in the owner's Drive and can be deleted there.

Contact

Via the Sandbox YouTube channel (@AtSandbox) About page.
